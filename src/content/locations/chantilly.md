## Our home town

We run out of Chantilly, so this is the one place where there is no drive to factor in.

Most of the housing here is from the late eighties onward, which means driveways, attached garages and walk-out basements. The truck gets close to the door and the job moves. Garage and basement clear-outs are straightforward here for that reason.

If your project involves demolition in an older structure, test for asbestos before you start. Most housing here is newer than that is a concern for, but some of the properties along the Route 50 corridor are considerably older.
