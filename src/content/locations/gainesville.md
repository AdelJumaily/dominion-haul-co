## Big houses, big garages, bigger loads

Homes out here are larger on average than most of our area, and they were built with the storage to match. Three-car garages in places, generous basements, walk-up attics, and lots with room for sheds and outbuildings.

The practical effect is simple. When a garage out here finally gets cleared, it is more often a full truck than the half a Centreville townhouse garage produces. Same job, more volume. Worth knowing before you get a quote.

Access itself is easy. Wide driveways, room to work, no tight streets.

## Basement projects

A lot of these homes were sold with unfinished basement space that is being built out now. The debris is predictable: drywall offcuts, framing lumber, packaging from flooring and fixtures.

The part people underestimate is everything that was being stored down there before the project could start. We can come back as the work generates more rather than waiting for one heap at the end, so the space stays usable.

Concrete and tile are priced by weight rather than by space, and we quote those on site.

## The edge of our map, honestly

Gainesville and Haymarket are the furthest points we cover, roughly 30 minutes out when I-66 cooperates, which it often does not.

So next morning is our standard offer out here, and we will take same-day work when the day's routing happens to allow it. We would rather give you a next-morning slot we will absolutely keep than a same-day window the interstate gets to veto.
