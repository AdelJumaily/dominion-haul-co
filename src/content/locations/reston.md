## The cluster carry

Reston was laid out around clusters, with shared parking courts and walkways and the cars kept away from the houses. It is a nice way to live and a specific kind of problem for anyone moving heavy things.

In practice the truck often parks a distance from your door and we carry from there, sometimes down a walkway and around a bend. We plan for it rather than being surprised by it. It changes how many people we bring and how long we are there. It does not change your price, because we quote by volume rather than by the hour.

## Town Center is a different job

The high-rises and mid-rises around Town Center work like Arlington buildings. Certificate of insurance for management, a booked freight elevator, set move hours. We can do all of it, but a Town Center building is not a same-day job, because the elevator booking alone usually needs a day or two.

## Booking

Give us the cluster name and the address. Reston is close to us, so weekday mornings are usually easy to get.
