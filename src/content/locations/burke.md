## What pathway access means

Burke Centre was laid out to cluster the housing, share the green space and keep cars at the edges, with walkways running between everything. Good place to live. Specific set of problems if you are carrying a couch.

A fair number of units are reachable only on foot from a shared parking area, and some back onto wooded walkways with no vehicle approach on that side at all. If yours is one of those, everything travels a distance on foot, sometimes down steps and around a bend.

We plan for it. It changes how many people we bring and how long we are there, and it is why we ask which conservancy you are in rather than just taking the street address. It does not change your price, because we quote by volume and not by the hour.

## The detached side

Not all of Burke is townhouses. Lake Braddock, Longwood Knolls, Signal Hill and Kings Park West are mostly detached homes on proper lots with garages, sheds and back yards. The truck gets to the driveway and those load quickly.

## Downsizing

If you are downsizing rather than clearing out, say so when you call. The job is different: you are keeping a curated fraction, you want a meaningful amount donated rather than dumped, and you are making a lot of decisions at once. We work keep, donate, haul, in that order.
