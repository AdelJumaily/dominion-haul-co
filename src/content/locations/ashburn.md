## Easy access, strict rules

Ashburn is newer housing, which from our side means wide driveways, big garages and walk-out basements. The truck gets close and the job moves quickly.

What shapes the day here is the HOAs. Most associations have something to say about where a work vehicle can park, what hours work can happen, and whether a vendor needs notice first. None of that is a problem. We park on the driveway, we work daytime hours, and nothing stays on your property after we leave.

That last part is usually why people here call us instead of renting a container. A dumpster in an Ashburn driveway normally needs written approval and has a limit on how long it can sit.

## Basement projects

A lot of homes here were sold with unfinished basement space that is being built out now. The debris is predictable: drywall offcuts, framing lumber, packaging from flooring and fixtures.

The part people underestimate is everything that was being stored down there before the project could start. That is often the bigger volume of the two. Worth mentioning when you get a quote.

## Booking

Weekday mornings are easiest. Saturdays fill up faster, so give us a few days' notice if you want one.
