## I-95 decides the schedule

The drive from Chantilly is roughly 40 minutes in the morning and can be an hour and a half or worse in the afternoon peak. Nothing about planning changes that.

So we run eastern Prince William early in the day and try to avoid a return leg through the evening. Book in the morning and same-day is realistic. If a hauler promises you a late-afternoon Friday arrival out here, the interstate is making that call, not them.

## Rental turnovers

If you are managing a unit from out of the area, this works remotely and it works well. Send photos, we agree on a price in writing, you leave a lockbox code or hand a key to the property manager, and we send photos of every room once it is empty. If you have several units turning over at once we will schedule them together.

## Occoquan

The town of Occoquan is small, historic and tightly built, with narrow streets and limited parking. It is workable and it needs planning. We stage the truck where we legally can and carry from there, so give us the exact address in advance.

## Appliances

Fridges, freezers and window AC units hold refrigerant, so they go to a recycler that recovers it rather than to general waste. We disconnect water lines on washers and dishwashers ourselves. Anything on a gas line needs a licensed gas fitter first.
