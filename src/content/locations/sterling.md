## Two halves, different jobs

Sterling Park is the older half. Modest single-family homes on flat lots with simple driveway access. The houses are not big, so a full clear-out here is often less volume than the same job in Ashburn. What comes out tends to be older: appliances that have been running in a basement for decades, furniture from a few owners back, and the contents of detached garages and backyard sheds.

Cascades, Countryside, Potomac Falls and Lowes Island are the newer half. Bigger houses, attached garages, finished and unfinished basements, HOA covenants. Those load like Ashburn jobs.

## Before you demolish

If you are renovating in the older part of Sterling, test for asbestos before you start taking things apart. Older flooring, the black adhesive underneath it, pipe insulation and textured ceilings are the usual places it turns up.

Intact it is generally fine. Broken up it is a real health issue rather than a paperwork one. We cannot legally haul it, and we would much rather you knew beforehand than after.
