## East and west are different drives

Eastern Loudoun covers Ashburn, Sterling, Brambleton and South Riding. That is close enough that we can usually get to you quickly on a weekday.

West of Leesburg takes longer. Purcellville is around 40 minutes each way in good traffic, and Route 7 through Leesburg at the wrong hour is not good traffic. The price out there is the same. What changes is scheduling, so for anything west of Leesburg we will generally offer you the next morning rather than squeeze it onto the end of a day.

## Booking

Morning slots are easier for us out here and more reliable for you. Give us the address when you call so we can work out the approach.
