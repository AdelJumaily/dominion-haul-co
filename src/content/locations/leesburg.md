## Working in the old town

The historic core is doable and it takes planning. Narrow streets, several of them one-way, limited and often restricted on-street parking, and a number of properties served by rear alleys that were sized for a cart rather than a truck.

So we stage at the nearest legal point and carry. Sometimes that is a short distance and sometimes it is not. We ask for the specific address when you book rather than just the town, because working the approach out in advance is the difference between a smooth morning and a truck blocking a one-way street.

The houses add their own constraints. Narrow interior staircases, low door headers, small landings with turns. Taking furniture apart is normal here rather than the exception.

## Outside the core

Lansdowne, Potomac Station, Exeter, Tavistock Farms and Woodlea Manor are newer development with wide streets, driveways and garages. Those are straightforward.

## Before you demolish anything

If your project involves removing part of the outside of a building in the historic district, that is a conversation with the town before work starts. We will flag it if we think yours needs one. Emptying a house, basement, attic or garage is just hauling.
