## Old Town access

The old core around the depot has the constraints you would expect from a nineteenth-century railroad town. Narrow streets, limited and sometimes restricted parking, and a number of properties served by rear alleys.

Houses there are small-roomed with tight staircases and low headers, so furniture comes apart in the room rather than going out whole. It is slower work and it does not affect your price, because we quote by volume rather than by the hour.

We ask for the specific address when you book so the approach is worked out in advance. A box truck improvising on a one-way street in Old Town is nobody's good morning.

## Independent cities

Manassas and Manassas Park are independent cities under Virginia law, not part of Prince William County, and they run their own refuse collection.

For you that mainly affects your alternatives rather than our work. City bulk collection exists and you already pay for it, so if what you have is one modest item you can get to the curb and you can wait for the schedule, use it. Check their current rules for limits. Where it stops working is volume, construction debris, appliances with refrigerant, and anything you cannot physically get down to the street.

## Booking

I-66 is the real scheduling variable, not the mileage. Morning is better for both of us.
