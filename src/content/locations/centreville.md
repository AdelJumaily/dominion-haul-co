## Close enough to be easy

Centreville is about ten minutes from us, which makes it one of the quickest areas we cover.

The housing is mostly mid-eighties through the nineties, so you get attached garages, unfinished basement storage space behind the finished part, and back yards with room for a shed or a play set. All of that loads easily. The truck gets to the driveway and we carry straight out.

## HOAs

Centreville is close to universally HOA-governed and the rules usually say something about where a work vehicle can sit and for how long. We park on your driveway, we work daytime hours, and nothing stays on the property after we leave. If you were weighing up a dumpster, that last part is the difference, since a container usually needs association approval and has a time limit on it.

## Booking

Saturdays are the busiest day for this kind of work, so give us a few days' notice if you want one. Weekday mornings are easier to get.
