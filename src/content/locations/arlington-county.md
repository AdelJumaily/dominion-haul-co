## Access is the whole job here

Arlington has two kinds of pickup and they barely resemble each other.

The high-rises along the Metro corridors and around National Landing are easy to load, but the building sets the terms: certificate of insurance, booked freight elevator, set hours. We can do all of that. We just cannot compress it.

The older rowhouses and brick colonials north of Route 50 are the opposite. No paperwork, but short shared driveways, permit parking, and basement stairs that were not built with a sleeper sofa in mind. Where there is an alley behind the house, the job gets a lot easier.

## What it costs

Same published prices as the rest of our area. Arlington jobs are often smaller simply because a condo holds less than a Fairfax basement does.
